OFFICIAL ALEN ASTERIS SOP

Midman Procedure

The standard flow we follow for every deal, from agreeing on a price to releasing funds. Follow it in order starting from step 1 — don't skip steps, so your transaction gets logged on the Trust Page.

01
Agree to the Deal in Front of the Midman
Buyer & seller lay out item details, price, and terms in the official midman group/chat — not private DMs. Anything agreed outside of this is not our responsibility.
02
Buyer Transfers Funds to the Midman
Funds are sent to the midman's account/e-wallet, not directly to the seller. The midman confirms receipt and issues a confirmation in chat.
03
Seller Sends the Item / Account
Once funds are confirmed, the seller sends the account/item complete with password change & recovery email as agreed at the start.
04
Buyer Checks & Verifies
The buyer gets a set window to check the item is complete (usually 10–15 minutes). If anything doesn't match the description, report it in chat before confirming completion.
05
Funds Released to the Seller
Once the buyer confirms everything's fine, the midman releases the funds to the seller. The deal is marked completed and automatically logged as a new star on the Trust Page.
06
If a Dispute Arises
As long as the midman still holds the funds, we act as mediator and work out a fair solution for both sides — funds won't be released unilaterally before an agreement is reached.
Rules
MANDATORY DURING THE PROCESS
Transactions outside the midman's oversight are not allowed (direct DMs, transfers outside the official account, etc).
Items already received & confirmed by the buyer cannot be returned, unless proven not to match the description.
Both parties must stay respectful. Spamming, flooding the chat, or intimidating staff/other parties is not allowed.
Don't skip any step — deals that don't follow the full procedure can't be guaranteed safe by us.
Account data (recovery email, old password) must be handed over in full during transfer — nothing may be withheld.
The item-checking window is limited as agreed at the start — passing the deadline without a complaint counts as deal completed.
