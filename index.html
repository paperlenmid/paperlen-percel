OFFICIAL PAPERLEN SOP

Midman Procedure

The standard flow we follow for every deal, from agreeing on a price to releasing funds. Follow it in order starting from step 1 — don't skip steps, so your transaction gets logged on the Trust Page.

01
GROUP CHAT
The Seller and Buyer Create A Group Chat With PAPERLEN As Middleman Exclusively On Whatsapp.
02
PAYMENT
The Buyer Sends The Payment, Including The Service Fee, To PAPERLEN.
03
CHECK AND SECURE
The Seller Sends The Game Account Details To PAPERLEN For Verification.
04
WITHDRAWAL
After The Account Has Been Verified And Secured, PAPERLEN Sends The Payment To The Seller.
05
RELEASE
PAPERLEN Releases The Account Details To The Buyer After The Seller Has Received The Payment.
Rules
MANDATORY DURING THE PROCESS
Transactions outside the midman's oversight are not allowed (direct DMs, transfers outside the official account, etc).
Items already received & confirmed by the buyer cannot be returned, unless proven not to match the description.
Both parties must stay respectful. Spamming, flooding the chat, or intimidating staff/other parties is not allowed.
Don't skip any step — deals that don't follow the full procedure can't be guaranteed safe by us.
Account data (recovery email, old password) must be handed over in full during transfer — nothing may be withheld.
The item-checking window is limited as agreed at the start — passing the deadline without a complaint counts as deal completed.
